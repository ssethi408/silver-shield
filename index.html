How accurate is this?

We trained a small word model on 70% of the UCI SMS Spam Collection and tested on the other 30%, which it never saw. Only a "Likely scam" answer counts as catching a scam.

98.3%accuracy on 1,672 held-out texts
10.7%of 224 spam texts not flagged
0.3%of 1,448 normal texts wrongly flagged
13 of 16current-style scams flagged (English, Spanish, Chinese)

What it cannot do. The training texts are from 2012 and mostly from the UK and Singapore. On a different collection of old spam emails it was right only 51% of the time (600 messages). It checks every link offline: whether it is a real web address and whether it uses look-alike names, short links or odd endings. It cannot see whether a site is live or where a short link leads, and it does not know the sender, so a polite scam with no warning words and no link can pass. A family-emergency text often gets "Cannot tell". It never says a message is safe.
